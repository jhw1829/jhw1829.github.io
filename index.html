Personal research utility · Hyeonu Jo

hyeonu-rclone

A private, non-commercial configuration for accessing my own Google Drive from my Ubuntu research server using the open-source rclone command-line tool.

Purpose

The connection enables remote reading of research datasets and writing analysis results without storing complete datasets on the server's local disk.

How Google Drive data is used

  • Access is authorized by the Google account owner through OAuth.
  • Drive files are read and written only as instructed by the account owner through rclone.
  • OAuth credentials are stored in the user's private server configuration, not on this website.
  • This site does not collect files, account information, or OAuth tokens.

This is a personal-use configuration, not a publicly offered file-storage service.
